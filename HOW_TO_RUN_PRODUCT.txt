[PHYSICAL HARDWARE PINOUT]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Connect 3 jumper wires between Arduino Nano and Digilent Arty S7-25:
  1. Arduino TX1 (Pin D1)  --->  Arty S7 Shield IO0 (Pin L13)  [FPGA RX Ingress]
  2. Arduino RX0 (Pin D0)  <---  Arty S7 Shield IO1 (Pin N13)  [FPGA TX Telemetry]
  3. Arduino GND           <--->  Arty S7 GND                  [Common Ground]
